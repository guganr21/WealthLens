          Privacy Notice

          WealthLens stores all financial information in your browser's LocalStorage. LocalStorage is not encrypted by default. Anyone with access to your device and browser profile may be able to view the stored data. Clearing browser data, using private/incognito mode, or uninstalling the browser may permanently erase your saved information. No financial data is transmitted to any external server.
